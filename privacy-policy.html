2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our services:

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use tracking cookies or analytics pixels

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver Your Assessment: Generate and email your dog's personalized behavior risk report
  • Email Communications: With your consent, send dog training tips and service information. You may unsubscribe at any time.
  • Phone/SMS Communications: Only if you provide a phone number AND grant explicit written consent. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP or visiting our unsubscribe page.
  • Service Improvement: Aggregate, anonymized data may be used to improve our assessment tool

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent: We process your email and phone data based on your explicit opt-in consent, documented with timestamp, IP address, and the exact consent language presented to you
  • Legitimate Interest: We process dog behavior data to deliver the assessment you requested

5. How We Store and Protect Your Data

  • Encryption in Transit: All data is transmitted over HTTPS using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Encryption at Rest: Data is stored in Supabase, which encrypts data at rest using AES-256
  • Access Control: Database access is restricted to server-side functions only; no direct public access to your data
  • Retention Period: Personal data is retained for up to 2 years from collection, or until you request deletion, whichever comes first
  • Consent Records: Consent audit records (timestamp, IP, consent language version) are retained for 5 years for legal compliance

6. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We use the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase: Secure cloud database hosting and serverless functions
  • Google Fonts: Font delivery (no personal data is shared with Google through this service)

7. AI-Generated Content Disclosure

The behavior assessment results you receive are generated by an algorithmic scoring system. Please understand:

  • Results are informational only and do not constitute professional veterinary, medical, or behavioral advice
  • Results do not guarantee specific outcomes from any training program
  • You should consult a qualified professional for serious behavioral concerns, especially those involving aggression
  • The assessment algorithm uses the information you provide (breed, age, behaviors, duration, frequency) to generate a risk score and recommendations

9. TCPA Compliance

We comply fully with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA):

  • You may revoke consent at any time by replying STOP, contacting us, or using our unsubscribe page
  • We check contacts against the National Do Not Call Registry before initiating outbound calls
  • All consent records (including timestamp, IP address, and exact consent language) are maintained for audit purposes

10. CAN-SPAM Compliance

  • Honor unsubscribe requests within 10 business days
